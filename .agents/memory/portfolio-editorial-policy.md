The second product's exact brand spelling is ANGARO, rendered as “Angaro”. The owner approved public descriptions after reviewing the actual private product repositories and documentation. Both products remain in development/pre-pilot and are not publicly launched.

**Why:** The owner corrected the brand spelling and supplied approved copy to replace an earlier neutral placeholder. Product descriptions explain design purpose, not verified completed features, customers, pilots, revenues or availability.

**How to apply:** Use the owner-approved copy without expanding it into unsupported claims. Do not edit either product's own codebase when working on this corporate website. Public portfolio removals should hide/archive internal records, not delete them; preserve historical snapshots even if their older wording differs.

The owner identified the official Generational Intelligence emblem as a Triskelion and supplied original company, GatherWell and Angaro logo files. Preserve those original artworks without redrawing, recolouring, distorting or cropping important marks.

**Why:** The owner explicitly required these particular supplied images, rather than generated or substitute branding.

**How to apply:** Use proportional image sizing and preserve original files and historic CMS records. For the ivory website, the owner explicitly approved evergreen backgrounds behind the white originals in the header and product logo panels.

**Why evergreen containment:** The original artwork is predominantly white and would lose important marks against light backgrounds; the owner chose background containment rather than replacement or recolouring.

The owner approved public links to both product websites while retaining “In Development” for both products.

**Why:** Reachable product websites do not establish that either software product has launched or is commercially available.

**How to apply:** Keep the development labels and purpose-qualified descriptions when maintaining these links; do not infer launch status from a successful website response.

Company/About information should be small and unobtrusive at the very bottom of the footer, not a large standalone section in the middle of the homepage.

**Why:** The owner said it “doesn't need to be this huge, big section” and asked for a little company information alongside the legal details.

**How to apply:** Preserve the footer-only About placement and its compact presentation in future website revisions unless the owner explicitly changes direction.

Website refinements must follow the owner's specified compact scale without creative additions or an independent redesign. Treat size limits as caps; do not enlarge elements already smaller.

**Why:** The owner repeatedly flagged oversized presentation and explicitly withheld creative discretion for the compact-scale refinement.

**How to apply:** Reuse the existing components and approved ivory/evergreen design, copy and artwork; do not introduce new visual treatments while making sizing corrections.
